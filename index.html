About us

A boutique consultancy with engineering at its core

Briskware delivers high-quality, scalable IT solutions. We blend strategic vision with engineering precision, and take responsibility for the outcome from first design through to production.

Our background is two decades of large-scale enterprise delivery, much of it in investment banking, where systems have to be correct, fast and auditable. We bring that discipline to every engagement, whatever the industry.

Case studies

Selected work

Anonymised highlights from past engagements. Select a case to see the challenge, our approach and the outcome.

  • Challenge
    Data about clients, employees and products was spread across many silos and loosely governed data lakes, with no shared definitions and little lineage.
    Approach
    We researched, architected and built an enterprise knowledge graph platform. Ontologies modelled the core business concepts, and identity and property resolution unified the sources under one graph. We worked with business architects, application owners and data architects across the organisation to define the data architecture for corporate governance, and engaged senior stakeholders and third-party vendors. The platform was fully containerised with CI/CD on in-house cloud infrastructure.
    Outcome
    Unstructured data lakes were retired in favour of a single governed graph. Strategic use cases went into production on it, including KYC, a 360-degree employee view and regulatory compliance.
    • Scala
    • Akka
    • RDF & OWL
    • SPARQL
    • SHACL
    • Docker
  • Challenge
    The client needed a single, trusted view of its legal entities and their relationships to serve several bespoke use cases, and was adopting knowledge graph and cloud technology for the first time.
    Approach
    We architected a new enterprise knowledge graph platform and were hands-on in building it: ontology design, a full-stack application, and SPARQL-based DataOps pipelines for loading and validating data. We owned DevOps end to end and guided the client's teams through adopting the technology.
    Outcome
    The platform runs in production on the client's OpenShift cluster, with cloud-based CI/CD on GKE. Reusable components were contributed back to the open-source knowledge graph community.
    • Ontology design
    • SPARQL
    • Kotlin
    • Python
    • React
    • OpenShift
    • GKE
  • Challenge
    Front-office order flow had to reach many downstream systems in real time, including middle and back office, compliance and regulatory reporting. It also had to extend from equities to exchange-traded derivatives without disrupting existing flows.
    Approach
    We extended the streaming order publisher, and designed and built FIX initiators and acceptors for each downstream "drop". We also worked on market data vendor connectivity and reference data streams. Delivery ran across several pods in a global agile team, with strict two-week sprints and a strong CI/CD culture.
    Outcome
    Derivatives flows went live alongside the existing equity flows, feeding bank-wide middle-office, back-office and regulatory consumers from a single streaming platform.
    • Java
    • Spring Boot
    • FIX
    • Streaming
    • Consul
    • Docker
  • Challenge
    An equity options pricing and volatility team needed option chains sourced from the market, configured and persisted reliably. At the same time, its legacy services had to move onto a new strategic platform.
    Approach
    We ported a legacy Spring Boot Java microservices stack to Kotlin on the strategic platform and extended its REST APIs. We designed and built a client for a real-time market data API, and a set of components to source, configure and persist option chains. Bash and Python harnesses handled orchestration and testing.
    Outcome
    We had full ownership from inception through staged delivery to production across APAC, EMEA and the Americas.
    • Kotlin
    • Spring Boot
    • Real-time market data
    • REST
    • Python
  • Challenge
    Reference data from many sources had to land reliably in an RDF-based reference data platform. Meanwhile, existing JVM services were running on ageing Java and Scala 2 versions.
    Approach
    We built bespoke ETL tooling, reference data ingestors and streamers, in Scala with ZIO and its ecosystem. We migrated existing services from Java and Scala 2 to Scala 3, and coached the wider team in Scala, functional programming, RDF and agile practices.
    Outcome
    The result is type-safe, testable ingestion pipelines, a codebase on a current Scala version, and a team more confident with functional programming and semantic data.
    • Scala 3
    • ZIO
    • RDF
    • ETL
    • Coaching

    Outcome
    A product was selected and a streamlined EDM platform designed and delivered to take over the master data responsibilities of the legacy platform.
    • EDM / MDM

    • Team leadership

  • Challenge
    A two-year transformation programme set out to restructure how an investment bank's internal systems exchanged data, spanning order management, trading, risk and fund administration platforms.
    Approach
    We provided enterprise integration architecture and development on an open-source EAI framework, building asynchronous messaging and web services with Spring on an application server and message bus. We designed and supported interfaces to the major trading and risk systems, and provided front-line support for the bank-wide platform. We also built and demonstrated a proof of concept for scalable, parallelised REST services in Scala and Akka over MongoDB.
    Outcome
    A bank-wide integration backbone supporting the transformation, and a demonstrated path towards reactive services for future interfaces.

    Approach

    An energy supplier had to put newly announced, government-backed customer support schemes live within weeks. The deadline was fixed, and the solution had to integrate with existing accounting and billing systems that were not designed for it.

Bespoke adaptors and plug-ins connected it to the accounting and billing systems over messaging and REST. Terraform, CircleCI and Flux gave fully automated pipelines from development to production on GKE.
    Outcome
    Live on time, meeting the government-mandated deadlines, with the same team providing first-line support after go-live.
